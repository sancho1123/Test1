### A. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT

**[1] U.S. missile defense stockpiles face a multi-year recovery gap following Operation Epic Fury and Ukraine support.**
The U.S. consumed more than 1,000 Patriot interceptors and an estimated 190–290 THAAD interceptors during Operation Epic Fury (Iran, Feb–Apr 2026). Combined with ongoing Ukraine support, current production cannot restore these inventories until mid-to-late 2029 under current ramp rates.

- **Confidence: High** (confirmed CSIS analysis + multiple corroborating reports)

Russia used 600 drones and 90 missiles, including an Oreshnik — a system capable of carrying a nuclear warhead — killing at least 2 and injuring 77+ in Kyiv. ### 3. DEFENSE ARTICLES, WEAPONS, AND STOCKPILES

#### A. U.S.-Provided Defense Articles

**Patriot Interceptors**
- **Status:** U.S. has consumed more than 1,000 PAC-3 interceptors in Operation Epic Fury alone. Only 172 Patriot interceptors were delivered in FY2026 — against more than 1,000 consumed. Ukraine continues to require Patriot interceptors to defend against Russian mass drone/missile attacks.
- **Replenishment:** Lockheed Martin 7-year deal (Jan 2026) to increase PAC-3 MSE from 600/year to 2,000/year by 2030. Restoration of pre-Epic Fury stockpile levels projected by mid-2029.
- **Source:** CSIS, [https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire](https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire) | Confidence: High
- **Ukraine impact:** Air defense is the critical gap. Russian mass drone-missile attacks are consuming interceptors faster than current delivery rates.
- **U.S. readiness impact:** HIGH. Multi-theater demand (Ukraine, Epic Fury replenishment, Indo-Pacific posture) against a supply constrained by production ramp timelines.
- **Congressional interest:** Yes — readiness and replenishment.
