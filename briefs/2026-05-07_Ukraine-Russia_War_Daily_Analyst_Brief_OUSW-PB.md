## PART I — SENIOR LEADER SNAPSHOT

### A. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT (BLUF)

**1. [Ceasefire / Battlefield]** Russia declared a unilateral 48-hour Victory Day ceasefire for May 8–9 while Ukraine simultaneously declared its own non-synchronized truce for May 5–6. Fighting continues. Russia killed at least 27 Ukrainian civilians in strikes on the Naftogaz gas facility and other targets in the days immediately preceding the announced ceasefire. The ceasefire dynamic is being monitored for negotiation signals.
- Source: Al Jazeera, "Russia and Ukraine declare competing ceasefires," May 4, 2026 | https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/4/russia-and-ukraine-declare-competing-ceasefires
- Confidence: **HIGH**

**2. [Defense Articles — FMS]** The U.S. State Department approved a $373.6M Foreign Military Sale (FMS) to Ukraine on May 5–6, 2026, covering 1,532 Joint Direct Attack Munition – Extended Range (JDAM-ER) guidance kits (1,200 KMU-572 for 2,000-lb bombs; 332 KMU-556 for 500-lb bombs). Boeing is the principal contractor. These munitions are intended for Ukraine's F-16 fleet and extend precision attack range to 72.4 km. This is the most significant defense article action of the reporting window.
- Source: U.S. State Department (Official), "Ukraine — Joint Direct Attack Munitions — Extended Range," May 5, 2026 | https://www.state.gov/releases/bureau-of-political-military-affairs/2026/05/ukraine-joint-direct-attack-munitions-extended-range
- Confidence: **HIGH (Official Source)**

**3. [Stockpiles / Epic Fury Linkage — CRITICAL]** Operation Epic Fury (U.S.-Israel campaign vs. Iran, Feb 28–May 5, 2026) consumed 850+ Tomahawk cruise missiles in four weeks — nine times the annual peacetime production rate of ~90/year. Ukraine's Patriot air defense systems are on confirmed "starvation ration." The U.S. warned European allies on May 2 of significant delays in weapons deliveries due to stockpile depletion from Epic Fury. HIMARS ammunition and NASAMS interceptors are also affected. This multi-theater stockpile competition is the most urgent issue for OUSW P/B analysts.
- Sources: Ukrainska Pravda, "US warns Europe of 'significant delays'," May 2, 2026 | https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/05/02/8032795/ | Breaking Defense | https://breakingdefense.com/2026/03/after-epic-fury-a-munitions-supplemental-becomes-imperative/ | Euromaidan Press, April 28, 2026 | https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/04/28/ukraines-patriot-systems-are-on-a-starvation-ration-air-force-spox-says/
- Confidence: **HIGH**

**4. [Funding / USAI — Execution Risk]** Defense Secretary Hegseth acknowledged the release of $400M in USAI FY2026 funds (late April 2026), following Sen. McConnell's public op-ed accusing the Pentagon of stonewalling. Pentagon Comptroller Jules Hurst III clarified funds are "not under contract, but released to be put under contract." This distinction creates significant execution risk and is a live congressional oversight issue.
- Source: The Hill | https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5856026-pentagon-releases-ukraine-aid/ | Bloomberg Government | https://news.bgov.com/bloomberg-government-news/pentagon-released-400-million-ukraine-funding-hegseth-says
- Confidence: **HIGH**

**5. [Epic Fury / Iran — Operation Concluded]** Secretary of State Rubio declared Operation Epic Fury "over" on May 5, 2026 (Day 67 of the conflict). A fragile ceasefire is in place. Strait of Hormuz disruptions drove Brent crude near $120/barrel at peak; prices remain elevated near $100/barrel as of early May. U.S. Navy Operation Project Freedom (tanker escort through Hormuz) launched May 4. Epic Fury's formal conclusion does not immediately resolve the CENTCOM-EUCOM munitions competition, as production ramp-up timelines are 2+ years.
- Sources: CNN | https://www.cnn.com/2026/05/05/world/live-news/iran-war-news | White House | https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/04/peace-through-strength-operation-epic-fury-crushes-iranian-threat-as-ceasefire-takes-hold/ | CNBC | https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/04/oil-prices-today-wti-brent-trump-iran-us-hormuz.html

**1. [USAI / Execution]** The $400M FY2026 USAI tranche is released but NOT yet under contract. Comptroller Hurst III's exact language: "not under contract, but released to be put under contract." Analysts must track obligation actions. NDAA FY2026 authorized $400M USAI FY26 + $400M USAI FY27.

- Likely RFI: *"When will JDAM-ERs actually arrive in Ukraine? Is Boeing's production line ready?"*
- Source: State Dept (Official) | https://www.state.gov/releases/bureau-of-political-military-affairs/2026/05/ukraine-joint-direct-attack-munitions-extended-range

Production ramp plans exist (PAC-3: 600→2,000/yr; THAAD: 96→400/yr) but require a 2-year lead time per INDOPACOM Commander Admiral Paparo. FY2026–FY2027 represents a critical gap year.
- Likely RFI: *"Can the U.S. simultaneously support EUCOM and CENTCOM air defense needs? What is the readiness risk?"*

NDAA FY2026 prohibits reclassifying Ukraine-contracted items as U.S. stocks and requires reporting on intelligence-support risks. Oversight risk is high.

### 11. ANALYST ASSESSMENT

**What matters most today:**
The single most consequential multi-theater development for OUSW Comptroller analysts is the confirmed competition between the post-Epic Fury resupply pipeline and Ukraine's air defense needs. Both theaters are drawing on the same depleted inventories of Patriot interceptors, NASAMS, and HIMARS ammunition. The U.S. DIB cannot surge production on a FY2026 timeline — ramp-ups for PAC-3 and THAAD are confirmed at 2+ years. 1. Whether the competing Russia-Ukraine ceasefires hold around May 8–9, and whether escalation follows their expiration
