## 4. DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL BASE AND PRODUCTION CAPACITY

### A. Lockheed Martin — PAC-3 MSE and THAAD

- **Issue:** Production agreements signed to ramp PAC-3 MSE from 600 to 2,000/year and THAAD from 96 to 400/year.
- **Challenge:** Additional production won't arrive for 3–4 years due to long lead times, component supply chains (guidance electronics, rocket motors, propellant), and workforce constraints.
- **Ukraine Impact:** No new PAC-3 transfers confirmed in this reporting window; interceptor scarcity is acute.
- **U.S. Readiness Impact:** THAAD battery crews in Europe and CENTCOM operating with significantly reduced reloads.
- Source: Army Recognition — https://www.armyrecognition.com/news/army-news/2026/u-s-to-quadruple-production-of-pac-3-thaad-and-tomahawk-missiles-after-iran-campaign
- Confidence: HIGH

### B. RTX (Raytheon Technologies) — Tomahawk and Patriot

- **Issue:** Framework agreement to increase TLAM production to 1,000+/year. Current production rate was insufficient to absorb 850+ missiles expended in 40 days.
- **Challenge:** Same propellant, guidance, and warhead component supply chains shared with PAC-3 and NASAMS.
- **Ukraine Impact:** NASAMS interceptors (supplied to Ukraine) use shared supply chains; Epic Fury may have affected NASAMS resupply timelines.
- **Comptroller Note:** Emergency procurement authorities may be required to accelerate production timelines without a supplemental appropriation.
- Source: Small Wars Journal — https://smallwarsjournal.com/2026/03/27/magazine-depth-iran-missiles-stockpile-readiness/
- Confidence: MEDIUM

### C. Ukraine Domestic DIB — Glide Bomb and Drone Production

- **Issue:** Ukraine declared its first domestic 250kg guided glide bomb combat-ready (May 18, 2026), produced by DG Industry via Brave1. Ukraine's domestic ammunition production hit $30B/year — 6x the prior year rate.
