§6. Operation Epic Fury / Iran and Ukraine–Russia War Linkage

A. Operational Background

Operation Epic Fury was a joint U.S.-Israeli military operation against Iran, active 28 February – 5 May 2026 (38 days). It achieved its core objectives: destruction of Iran's ballistic missile arsenal and launch capacity (85%+ of Iran DIB), elimination of Iran's naval threat, disruption of proxy networks, and prevention of Iran nuclear acquisition. A ceasefire and MoU were signed. Iran agreed to reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
Sources: White House, Apr 2026 | DoD Fact Sheet, Mar 2026

B. Weapons and Stockpile Competition (CONFIRMED LINKAGE)

CONFIRMED: Operation Epic Fury consumed U.S. interceptors at a rate that directly degraded the pool available for Ukraine. In 38 days, Epic Fury used 850+ Tomahawk cruise missiles, 1,000+ Patriot and THAAD interceptors, and 1,300+ Army Tactical Ballistic Missiles. These are the same systems Ukraine urgently needs. The production timeline to replace them (mid-2028 for Patriot) means the multi-theater readiness gap will persist for at least 2 years.
Source: Global Research, "Iran/Ukraine Drained Global Patriot Stockpile," 2026 | FPRI, May 2026

C. Iran → Russia Weapons Pipeline (Disrupted)

Iran had transferred thousands of Shahed-series drones and ballistic missiles to Russia prior to Epic Fury. Russia was reportedly coaching Iran on drone tactics (altitudes, salvo sizes). Epic Fury's destruction of 85%+ of Iran's DIB has severed or severely degraded this pipeline. North Korean and Chinese inputs are partially compensating. This is a net positive for Ukraine's air defense burden, though the effect is gradual.
Source: The Diplomat, Aug 2026 | CSIS, CRINK, 2026 MEDIUM

D. Post-Epic Fury Residual Risks

Iran resumed attacks on commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz (3 vessels struck, July 6–7, 2026). This raises the risk of a renewed CENTCOM response, further depleting shared U.S. munitions stockpiles. If CENTCOM requires additional interceptor deployments, Ukraine's supply prioritization could be directly impacted. The ceasefire MoU has not held fully.
Source: Quwa, 2026 | Manara Magazine, Apr 2026
