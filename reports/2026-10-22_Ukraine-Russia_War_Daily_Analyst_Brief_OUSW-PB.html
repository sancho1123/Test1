  • Iranian Shahed Drones in Ukraine [CONFIRMED HIGH]: Iran has transferred thousands of Shahed-series drones to Russia, which uses them in mass attacks against Ukrainian infrastructure. C. Russia–Iran Relationship

  • Operation Epic Fury Effect on Russia-Iran Cooperation: Analyst Judgment. Operation Epic Fury places direct military and economic pressure on Iran. However, Iran–Russia military cooperation (drones, missile technology, financial networks) predates Epic Fury and appears to continue despite it. The financial intelligence community (bne IntelliNews, Oct 2026) reports that the wars in Iran and Ukraine are actually accelerating CRINK technology transfer. bne IntelliNews
  • Iran Drone Supply to Russia: Confirmed. Thousands of Shahed drones transferred. A Washington Institute analysis notes Moscow switched to DPRK as its primary ammunition supplier but continues relying on Iran for drone/missile technology.
  • Does Epic Fury weaken Russia's supply chain? Cannot fully confirm. Epic Fury may constrain Iran's ability to ramp up production, but Russia has diversified to DPRK ammunition, reducing dependence. Russia benefits from U.S. strategic distraction in two theaters. Analyst Judgment.
