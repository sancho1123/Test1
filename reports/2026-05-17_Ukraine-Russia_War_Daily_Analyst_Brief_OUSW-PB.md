**[Stockpiles / Readiness]** U.S. Patriot and THAAD interceptor stockpiles are under severe dual-theater pressure. Epic Fury consumed an estimated 40–80% of the U.S. global THAAD inventory (534 total rounds) and burned through Patriot interceptors at a rate equivalent to 18 months of combined Lockheed/Boeing production in just the first four days. ## 4. DEFENSE INDUSTRIAL BASE AND PRODUCTION CAPACITY

### 4A. Patriot/THAAD Production — Critical Bottleneck

- **Lockheed Martin / Boeing PAC-3 MSE:** Production capacity is approximately 500–600 interceptors per year combined. Epic Fury consumed ~943 in four days. Current production cannot meet simultaneous U.S., NATO, and Ukrainian demand.
- **THAAD (Lockheed Martin):** Production rate is slow; radars (AN/TPY-2) take years to manufacture. One of nine destroyed in Jordan; replacement timeline is unclear.
- **Analyst note:** Multi-year procurement authorities and emergency production contracts will likely be required and may generate congressional action.
  > Source: Military Times, "'Race of attrition'," Mar 2026. Link: https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2026/03/06/race-of-attrition-us-militarys-finite-interceptor-stockpile-is-being-tested/

### 4B. 155mm Artillery — Production Gap Persists

- Army goal: 100,000 rounds/month (originally by Oct 2025)
- Actual: ~40,000/month (as of current reports — same as September 2024)
- DoD invested ~$2 billion to drive a 7x increase; results not yet materializing
- Ukrainian-U.S. joint propellant facility (D&M Holding, Florida) expected to begin production mid-2026
  > Source: Federal News Network, Mar 2026. Link above.

### 4C. Iran's Defense Industrial Base — Eliminated as Russian Supply Source

## 6. OPERATION EPIC FURY / IRAN AND UKRAINE–RUSSIA WAR LINKAGE

### 6A. Overview

Operation Epic Fury was the U.S. code name for joint U.S.-Israel military operations against Iran, commencing 28 February 2026 and concluding 5 May 2026.
> Source: White House, "Peace Through Strength: Operation Epic Fury Crushes Iranian Threat as Ceasefire Takes Hold," Apr 2026. Link: https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/04/peace-through-strength-operation-epic-fury-crushes-iranian-threat-as-ceasefire-takes-hold/

### 6B. Weapons and Stockpile Competition — CONFIRMED DIRECT LINKAGE

**This is the most consequential cross-theater linkage in today's reporting.**

Both conflicts drew from the same U.S. and allied interceptor inventories simultaneously:

- PAC-3 MSE interceptors used in both theaters (Epic Fury + Ukraine)
- U.S. consumed ~18 months of Patriot production in the first 4 days of Epic Fury
- THAAD interceptors: 40–80% of global inventory expended in Epic Fury; **none transferred to Ukraine but global inventory depletion removes any near-term option**
- GCC states burned through ~86% of their pre-war Patriot-family inventory — reducing any reallocation options from allies
- Ukraine is attempting to source Patriot missiles from Gulf states via drone-defense barter arrangements, reflecting the acute shortage

> Source: The Hill, "US used nearly half of Patriot missile stockpile in Iran campaign: Analysis." Link: https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5842118-patriot-thaad-prsm-expenditure-iran/
> Confidence: **HIGH** — confirmed across multiple credible defense analysis sources

**Bottom line for OUSW P/B:** The interceptor depletion from Epic Fury is directly constraining the rate and quantity of air defense support to Ukraine. This will generate congressional RFIs about prioritization, readiness, and replenishment funding.

**Confirmed:**

- **Analyst note (judgment):** The waiver lapse simultaneously tightens sanctions pressure on Russia AND may contribute to modest global oil price movement. It is the first signal that the administration considers Epic Fury energy market disruption effects sufficiently resolved.
