Operation Epic Fury expended approximately two-thirds of the U.S. pre-war Patriot inventory against Iranian missiles and drones. This inventory has not been replenished and directly competes with Ukraine's air defense needs.   • THAAD: Demand elevated post-Epic Fury. Quadrupling production authorized. 53-month timeline. Supply constrained. HIGH
