## A. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT (BLUF)

**What changed in the last 24–48 hours. What matters most today.**

---

**[Air Defense / Defense Articles]** Russia executed its largest combined drone-missile strike of 2026 overnight on June 1–2, launching **656 drones and 73 missiles** at Ukraine. Ukrainian air defenses downed 40 missiles and 602 drones. **22 civilians were killed; dozens wounded.** Primary targets: Kyiv, Dnipro, Poltava, Kharkiv, and Zaporizhzhia. President Zelenskyy renewed his urgent request to President Trump for Patriot interceptors.
> Source: Al Jazeera, "At least 22 people killed, dozens wounded in Russian attacks on Ukraine," June 2, 2026 — https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/2/at-least-nine-people-killed-dozens-wounded-in-russian-attacks-on-ukraine
> **Confidence: HIGH** — Confirmed by multiple credible outlets and Ukrainian government.

---

**[Stockpiles / Epic Fury Linkage]** Operation Epic Fury (Feb 28–May 5, 2026) consumed **~45% of U.S. Patriot PAC-3 interceptors**, **50% of THAAD**, **30% of Tomahawks**, and **20%+ of long-range JASSMs**. CSIS assessed replenishment timelines of **3–5 years** for key systems. Ukraine's air defense interceptor supply is directly constrained by the Iran war draw-down. Russia is actively exploiting the U.S. interceptor shortage.
> Source: CSIS, "Last Rounds? Status of Key Munitions at the Iran War Ceasefire," 2026 — https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire
> **Confidence: HIGH** — Multiple official and credible analytical sources.

---

**[Funding / Appropriations]** The FY2026 NDAA provides only **$400M USAI** for Ukraine (vs. $14B in the FY2024 supplemental). The House Appropriations bill includes **$0 USAI**; the Senate provides **$800M**. Conference has not reconciled. Presidential Drawdown Authority (PDA) remains suspended under Trump's second term. Ukraine's air defense funding and equipment pipeline is under significant pressure.
> Source: OSW Centre for Eastern Studies, "US defence budget for 2026," Dec 19, 2025 — https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-19/us-defence-budget-2026-congress-approves-continued-support-ukraine
> **Confidence: HIGH** — Official congressional and DoD sources.

---

**[DIB / Readiness]** The U.S. defense industrial base cannot rapidly replenish depleted stocks. Tomahawk production consumed 9× annual peacetime output in four weeks during Epic Fury. DOD is reportedly developing an emergency funding request potentially totaling **$50 billion** for replenishment. Competing demands from Ukraine, Epic Fury aftermath, and Indo-Pacific readiness create a multi-theater readiness crisis.
> Source: Federal News Network, "Billions are flowing into munitions, but there are limits," March 2026 — https://federalnewsnetwork.com/defense-main/2026/03/billions-are-flowing-into-munitions-but-there-are-limits-to-how-quickly-the-u-s-can-replenish-its-stockpiles/
> **Confidence: HIGH** — Confirmed by CSIS, CRS, and DoD-adjacent reporting.

---

**[Epic Fury / Iran Linkage]** Epic Fury disrupted Iran's ability to supply Russia with Shahed drones and ballistic missiles. However, **Russia had already moved ~90% of Shahed assembly inside Russia** prior to the conflict and developed the **Garpiya-3** (an improved Shahed variant, built with Chinese specialist assistance at a Russian-Chinese facility). Iran's disruption provides **only partial, temporary relief** on Russia's drone supply chain.
> Source: FPRI, "From Tehran to Donbas: What the Iran War Means for Russia and Ukraine," March 2026 — https://www.fpri.org/article/2026/03/from-tehran-to-donbas-what-the-iran-war-means-for-russia-and-ukraine/
> **Confidence: MEDIUM** — Well-sourced analysis; 90% domestic transfer figure from prior intelligence reporting.

## B. TOP ISSUES FOR OUSW COMPTROLLER P/B ANALYSTS

**Tagged for budget and oversight relevance.**

---

**[Defense Articles / Stockpiles]** Patriot PAC-3 interceptors are the most acute near-term shortfall. The U.S. used 1,300+ Patriot interceptors during Epic Fury — nearly half its inventory. Ukraine simultaneously faces record Russian missile and drone barrages. Zelenskyy has made two urgent requests to Trump (late May and June 2, 2026) for Patriot resupply. There is no confirmed U.S. commitment to supply additional interceptors at this time.
> *RFI Risk: Congress will likely ask: Are Patriot stocks sufficient to meet both deterrence and Ukraine commitments?*

---

**[USAI / Appropriations]** FY2026 USAI is at $400M (NDAA-enacted). House Appropriations appropriated $0. Senate appropriated $800M. **This gap is unresolved.** Until a final appropriations bill is enacted, Ukraine's FY2026 USAI pipeline remains capped at the NDAA authorization level. Analysts should flag: if the House position holds in conference, USAI drops to zero for the remainder of FY2026 above the NDAA baseline.
> *RFI Risk: Congress will ask: How much USAI remains available? What happens if the House bill prevails?*

---

**[PDA / Execution]** PDA has been suspended since Trump's second term began. A single $300M PDA package was publicly announced in 2025; none have been confirmed in 2026. Prior Biden-era PDA packages (55 packages / $31.7B) are largely exhausted. Unless new PDA is authorized, the direct-from-stockpile pipeline to Ukraine is effectively closed. This is the primary execution gap.
> *RFI Risk: Congress will ask: Is PDA being used? What authority exists to resume it?*

---

**[Replenishment / DIB]** DOD's emergency replenishment funding request (~$50B) is in development. Tomahawk production: only 207 TLAMs delivered in 2026 vs. 1,000+ expended in Epic Fury (9× annual production in four weeks). THAAD and Patriot replenishment: 3+ years. Congressional action on emergency supplemental funding or multi-year procurement is necessary to close the gap.
> *RFI Risk: What emergency authorities does DOD have? What is the funding gap to restore readiness?*

---

**[Congressional RFI / Readiness]** New START expired February 5, 2026, removing all treaty limits on U.S. and Russian strategic nuclear weapons for the first time since 1972. U.S. intelligence assessed in 2026 that "the most dangerous threat posed by Russia is an escalatory spiral" in Ukraine. Reduced U.S. intelligence focus on Russia (per Belfer Center reporting) during Epic Fury represents a strategic attention risk.
> *RFI Risk: Congress will ask: Has U.S. intelligence on Russia been degraded? What is the escalation risk?*

---

**[Epic Fury / Iran Linkage]** Post-Epic Fury energy disruption — Hormuz closure, Brent crude peaking above $120/barrel, EU gas prices up 70% — has forced European allies to import record volumes of **Russian LNG** (£3B Jan–Apr 2026). This paradoxically **increases Russian energy revenue** at a time when European allies are being asked to increase Ukraine burden-sharing. It complicates fiscal capacity arguments for allied contributions.
> *RFI Risk: Does the Hormuz crisis undermine allied fiscal capacity for Ukraine? Is Russian LNG revenue funding the war?*

---

**[Funding / Coalition]** Ukraine's partners pledged $38B in military aid at the Ukraine Defence Contact Group (UDCG) on February 12, 2026. European commitments are bearing more of the burden as U.S. direct contributions have declined sharply. The EU is facing its own fiscal stress from energy costs. Burden-sharing analysis will be a likely senior leader and congressional topic.
> *RFI Risk: What share of Ukraine's $38B coalition pledge has been delivered? What is the U.S. share?*
