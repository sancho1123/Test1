# PART I — SENIOR LEADER SNAPSHOT

## A. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT

**[1] [Defense Articles / Stockpiles] Russia has intensified its aerial campaign to record levels while Patriot interceptor shortages have reached a critical state.**
Ukraine consumed ~700 Patriot interceptors in a single winter (≈ annual U.S. production). Zelenskyy publicly stated current air defenses cannot intercept a significant share of incoming missiles. Russia launched a record 8,150 long-range drones in May (+24% from April); the June 2 barrage alone used 73 missiles + 656 drones, killing 22.
*Source: [Guardian/Pravda NATO, June 3, 2026](https://nato.news-pravda.com/world/2026/06/03/106685.html) | [PBS NewsHour, June 2, 2026](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/massive-russian-attack-on-kyiv-and-other-ukrainian-cities-kills-22-people-officials-say-as-moscow-escalates-fighting)*
**Confidence: HIGH**

**[2] [Epic Fury / Iran Linkage / Readiness] Operation Epic Fury directly depleted U.S. Patriot, THAAD, and Tomahawk inventories — creating a multi-theater competition for the same interceptors Ukraine urgently needs.**
CSIS confirmed the U.S. expended >50% of prewar Patriot inventory in 39 days. Every PAC-3 MSE rolling off the Camden, AR line is allocated to CENTCOM reconstitution and INDOPACOM deterrence — not Ukraine. Rebuilding will take 3–5 years.
*Source: [CSIS, "Last Rounds?" 2026](https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire) | [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/us-rearms-iran-ceasefire-advanced-munitions-supplies/)*
**Confidence: HIGH**

**[3] [Appropriations / USAI / Congressional RFI] FY2026 USAI funding is unresolved and contested: House $0, Senate $800M, NDAA authorized $400M, zero new PDA packages under Trump.**
Pentagon sought a "reduction" in Ukraine spending (~June 10). Senator McConnell publicly criticized the Pentagon for sitting on $400M in authorized aid.
*Source: [OSW](https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-19/us-defence-budget-2026-congress-approves-continued-support-ukraine) | [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/5854101-mitch-mcconnell-criticizes-pentagon-ukraine/)*
**Confidence: HIGH**

**[4] [Escalation Risk] North Korea has institutionalized its military alliance with Russia via a 2027–31 cooperation treaty.**
~14,000–15,000 North Korean troops have served in theater; ~6,000 KIA/WIA. North Korean troops marched at Moscow's Victory Day parade (May 2026). North Korea supplied millions of artillery shells and ballistic missiles.
*Source: [Kyiv Independent](https://kyivindependent.com/nearly-11-000-north-korean-troops-stationed-in-russias-kursk-oblast-at-start-of-2026-media-reports/) | [Atlantic Council](https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/ukrainealert/north-korea-is-using-russias-ukraine-invasion-to-upgrade-its-army/)*
**Confidence: HIGH**

**[5] [Sanctions] China now supplies 90% of Russia's sanctioned technology imports and ~80% of components for Russian weapons production.**
EU adopted its 20th sanctions package in April 2026 targeting Chinese entities; enforcement remains insufficient. U.S. "50% rule" enforcement set for November 2026.
*Source: [Bloomberg/Moscow Times, May 1, 2026](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/05/01/china-now-supplies-90-of-russias-sanctioned-tech-imports-bloomberg-a92662)*

## 6. OPERATION EPIC FURY / IRAN AND UKRAINE–RUSSIA WAR LINKAGE

> **CONFIRMED DIRECT LINKAGE:** Operation Epic Fury has directly depleted U.S. Patriot and THAAD interceptor inventories and redirected all new PAC-3 MSE production away from Ukraine. This is the most significant cross-theater defense article impact in the current reporting period.

### A. Weapons and Stockpile Competition — CONFIRMED

- **Patriot:** ~50% U.S. prewar Patriot inventory expended in Epic Fury's 39-day active campaign. Every new PAC-3 MSE → CENTCOM reconstitution + INDOPACOM deterrence. Ukraine receives none. | [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire) | [DefenceUkraine](https://www.defenceukraine.com/en/insights/ukraine-air-defence-pivot-2026/)
- **THAAD:** Depleted by Epic Fury; 3+ years to rebuild; unavailable for Ukraine. | [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/rebuilding-us-missile-inventory-multiyear-project)
- **TLAM:** >1,000 fired in Epic Fury; <200/yr production; no rebuild to prewar until late 2030. | [Military Times](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/pentagon-congress/2026/05/27/us-munitions-depleted-by-iran-war-will-take-years-to-restore-analysis-finds/)
- **WaPo (Mar 26):** U.S. was considering diverting Ukraine-bound aid to the Middle East. | [WaPo](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/03/26/us-iran-war-ukraine-missile-defense/)
- **Politico (via Ukranews):** Ukraine may face shortage of Patriot specifically due to the Middle East war. | [Ukranews](https://ukranews.com/en/news/1139572-ukraine-may-face-a-shortage-of-patriot-missiles-due-to-new-war-in-the-middle-east-politico)

**Key answers:**
- Are both conflicts drawing from the same inventory? **YES — confirmed.** Patriot, THAAD.
- Is one affecting the other's supply priority? **YES — Epic Fury has displaced Ukraine as a Patriot supply priority.**
- Are replenishment timelines affected? **YES — 3–5 years for key systems.**

- The fragility of the Epic Fury ceasefire (Iran retains ~70% ballistic missile arsenal; underground production intact) is the single largest risk factor to the U.S. air-defense inventory situation.
- Russia's continued Shahed-type drone campaign at scale suggests stockpiles in Russia remain significant regardless of Epic Fury's effect on Iranian production.
- The 2027–31 Russia-DPRK cooperation framework has implications that extend beyond Ukraine and the current conflict.
