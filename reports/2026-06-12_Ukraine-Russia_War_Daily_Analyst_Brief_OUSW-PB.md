# PART I — SENIOR LEADER SNAPSHOT

## A. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT (BLUF)

**[Air Defense / Stockpiles / Epic Fury Linkage — CRITICAL]**
Operation Epic Fury (Feb 28 – May 5, 2026) consumed 402 Patriot PAC-3 interceptors in its first 16 days. Gulf state Patriot stockpiles (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Israel) were drawn down approximately 86%. U.S. THAAD inventory was depleted ~40%. Washington is now explicitly prioritizing CENTCOM reconstitution and INDOPACOM deterrence over Ukraine resupply. Russia has already exploited this window.
- Source: Defence Ukraine, "The Patriot Runs Out: Ukraine's Air-Defence Pivot," 2026 — https://www.defenceukraine.com/en/insights/ukraine-air-defence-pivot-2026/
- Confidence: **HIGH**

**[Battlefield — June 2 Mass Strike]**
Russia fired 73 missiles and 656 drones at Kyiv and other major cities on June 2 — its third heavy assault in less than a month — killing at least 22 civilians including children, injuring ~140, and collapsing an apartment building in Dnipro. Analysts explicitly linked the attack to depleted U.S. air defense stocks resulting from Operation Epic Fury.
- Source: NBC News, "Russia unleashes deadly drone, missile attack on Ukraine as Iran war drains air defenses," June 2026 — https://www.nbcnews.com/world/ukraine/russia-drone-missile-attack-ukraine-kyiv-iran-war-air-defenses-rcna332101
- Source: PBS News, "Massive Russian attack on Kyiv and other Ukrainian cities kills 22," June 2026 — https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/massive-russian-attack-on-kyiv-and-other-ukrainian-cities-kills-22-people-officials-say-as-moscow-escalates-fighting
- Confidence: **HIGH**

**[Defense Articles — June 10 European Patriot Initiative]**
Ukraine is now in a "critical state of vulnerability" due to Patriot interceptor shortages. On June 10, Ukraine, the UK, France, and Germany announced a joint program to develop a "European Patriot" alternative (Ukraine's Fire Point company + Germany's Diehl Defence). Earliest fielding: end-of-decade (~2030). Current equivalent production: approximately 1 interceptor per month.
- Source: The Telegraph via Pravda UK, "Britain Will Help Ukraine Create a 'European Patriot'," June 10, 2026 — https://uk.news-pravda.com/world/2026/06/10/149191.html
- Confidence: **MEDIUM** (The Telegraph report; UK MoD not yet confirmed independently)

**[Funding — June 10 Ukraine Budget Amendment]**
Ukraine's parliament approved a record +UAH 1.56 trillion ($34.7 billion) defense spending increase on June 10, funded by the EU €90 billion loan backed by frozen Russian assets. Ukraine's total 2026 defense budget now stands at approximately $97.2 billion, with $51.3 billion earmarked for military equipment purchases alone. EU distributing €3.2 billion this month.
- Source: Global Banking and Finance, "Ukrainian Lawmakers Approve Record Defence Spending in 2026 Budget," June 10, 2026 — https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/ukrainian-lawmakers-pass-budget-changes-record-defence/
- Confidence: **HIGH**

Russian Deputy FM Galuzin stated Putin is prepared to use nuclear weapons if Russia or Belarus face security risks.

## B. TOP ISSUES FOR OUSW COMPTROLLER P/B ANALYSTS

1. **[Stockpiles / Readiness / Epic Fury Linkage — CRITICAL]**
Operation Epic Fury consumed more Patriot interceptors in 16 days than the annual PAC-3 production capacity can replace. The U.S. cannot simultaneously support Ukraine, reconstitute CENTCOM, and maintain INDOPACOM deterrence without significant new production investment or emergency reprogramming. Russia has already exploited this once (June 2 attack).
*What Congress may ask:* "Are we able to meet Ukraine's Patriot needs given Epic Fury consumption, and what is the replenishment timeline?"

2. **[Appropriations / USAI / Execution Risk]**
FY2026 USAI funding: NDAA authorized $400M; House appropriations bill provides $0 (not requested by Trump Administration, committee effort to add funding failed); Senate provides $800M. Conference resolution unresolved. The gap between authorization and appropriation creates direct execution risk for DoD's Ukraine assistance programs.
*What Congress may ask:* "Is there an enacted USAI appropriation for FY2026, and if not, under what authority is DoD providing Ukraine assistance?"
- Source: OSW Centre for Eastern Studies, "US defence budget for 2026," Dec 19, 2025 — https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-19/us-defence-budget-2026-congress-approves-continued-support-ukraine

3. **[Defense Articles / Replenishment — Structural Gap]**
Ukraine is "critically dependent" on U.S.-supplied Patriot systems per Ukrainian officials. The European alternative won't be available until ~2030. The Germany-led procurement of 35 PAC-3 interceptors represents approximately 3 launcher reloads—covering only a small number of engagement cycles against Russia's saturation salvos of Iskander-M, Kinzhal, and Kalibr. There is a structural air defense gap that no current program can fill before the war ends or major new investment is made.
*What Congress may ask:* "What is DoD's plan to address Ukraine's near-term air defense gap given production constraints and Epic Fury depletion?"

4. **[DIB / 155mm Production Shortfall]**
The U.S. Army is currently producing 40,000 155mm artillery rounds per month against a target of 100,000 per month by mid-2026. Historical Ukrainian requirements have exceeded 100,000 per month. The Army had to restart domestic TNT production (halted since 1986) and identify alternative propellant sources. Congress provided $25.9B supplemental for replenishment; DoD has requested an additional $18B.
*What Congress may ask:* "Is the 155mm production target on track for mid-2026, and is the additional $18B supplemental request still pending?"
- Source: National Defense Magazine — https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2025/8/14/army-falls-short-of-155mm-production-goal

5. **[Epic Fury / CENTCOM / Multi-Theater Demand]**
Operation Epic Fury's ceasefire (April 8, 2026) remains fragile. Iran refuses to reopen the Hormuz Strait. CENTCOM remains in reconstitution mode. Any re-escalation with Iran would further drain shared interceptor inventories. The three-way resource competition (EUCOM/Ukraine + CENTCOM + INDOPACOM) has no enacted budget solution.
*What Congress may ask:* "Are CENTCOM reconstitution requirements preventing adequate Ukraine air defense support, and what does DoD need to resolve this?"

6. **[Funding / EU Burden Sharing]**
The EU's €90 billion loan backed by frozen Russian assets is the largest single new Ukraine funding mechanism of 2026. €3.2 billion is being distributed in June 2026. This reduces but does not eliminate Ukrainian dependence on U.S. direct aid. It also increases congressional interest in whether Europe is now carrying more of the burden.
*What Congress may ask:* "What is the current U.S.-to-Europe burden-sharing ratio for Ukraine defense funding, and has it shifted in FY2026?"

7. **[Congressional RFI / North Korea / Sanctions]**
Approximately 11,000 North Korean troops remain in Russia's Kursk Oblast with ~6,000 casualties. Russia has deployed North Korean Hwasong-11 SRBMs and millions of artillery shells in Ukraine. Approximately 3,000 NKPA combat veterans have already returned to North Korea as military instructors, exporting modern battlefield experience back to Pyongyang.
*What Congress may ask:* "What is the current scale and operational role of North Korean military support to Russia, and how does it affect Ukraine's battlefield position?"

The confirmed depletion of U.S. Patriot interceptor stockpiles as a direct result of Operation Epic Fury — combined with Russia's demonstrated exploitation of Ukraine's resulting "window of vulnerability" in the June 2 mass strike (73 missiles + 656 drones, 22 civilians killed) — is the single most operationally significant finding in this report. 1. Congressional action on USAI FY2026 appropriations conference
2. Any DoD replenishment request explicitly linked to Epic Fury Patriot/THAAD depletion
3. Iran Hormuz Strait status; risk of CENTCOM re-escalation and further interceptor demand
4. UK/France/Germany "European Patriot" funding commitments
5. Russian offensive tempo in Slovyansk, Kupyansk directions

### What could affect budget execution

USAI conference outcome; any emergency PDA or reprogramming for Patriot replenishment; potential new DoD supplemental for Epic Fury-related stockpile restoration. The $18B additional replenishment request not yet enacted is a live execution risk.

### What is the most important cross-theater linkage

The Patriot interceptor competition between Ukraine (EUCOM) and CENTCOM reconstitution is the dominant and confirmed cross-theater linkage in this reporting window. Russia has already exploited it once. Continued exploitation is the most likely near-term escalation vector.
