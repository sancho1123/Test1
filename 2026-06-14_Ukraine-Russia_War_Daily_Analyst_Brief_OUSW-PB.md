## B. TOP ISSUES FOR OUSW COMPTROLLER P/B ANALYSTS

**1. [Defense Articles / Stockpiles / Readiness] PATRIOT INTERCEPTOR CRISIS**
U.S. consumed 1,000+ PAC-3 MSE interceptors in Operation Epic Fury. U.S. took delivery of only 172 PAC-3 MSEs in FY2026. NDAA FY2026 (signed by Trump) authorized $400M USAI. Senate appropriations bill: $800M USAI + $225M Baltic Security Initiative. House appropriations bill: $0 Ukraine-specific funding. Senate panel advancing additional $1B Ukraine bill. No new PDA usage since January 2025. Conference outcome will determine FY2026 executable balance.
- Source: OSW Centre for Eastern Studies, Dec 2025 | https://www.osw.waw.pl/en/publikacje/analyses/2025-12-19/us-defence-budget-2026-congress-approves-continued-support-ukraine

As of October 2024, DoD obligated only 55% ($25.1B of $45.8B) of PDA replenishment appropriations. With Epic Fury now adding CENTCOM demand, replenishment contracts face direct competition for limited production capacity.
