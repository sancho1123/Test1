### 3D. Defense Article Analysis — OUSW P/B Key Questions

**Q: What defense article is most urgently needed?**
A: Patriot PAC-3 MSE interceptors are the most critical unmet need. Ukraine has exhausted what it received, it has no counter to Oreshnik, and no new deliveries are confirmed. Secondary: 155mm rounds, long-range strike munitions.

**Q: Is there a possible reprogramming or execution implication?**
A: Yes. The $400M USAI authorized in the NDAA could technically be used for air defense interceptors, but: (1) the funds are not appropriated, (2) SecDef controls execution, and (3) delivery lead times make near-term impact unlikely even if a decision were made today.

**Q: Does this create congressional interest?**
