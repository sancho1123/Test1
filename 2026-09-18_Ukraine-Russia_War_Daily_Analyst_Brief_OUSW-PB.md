## 3. Defense Articles, Weapons, Stockpiles, and Munitions

### A. PURL — New Mechanism (CRITICAL for Analysts)

The **Prioritized Ukraine Requirements List (PURL)** is a NATO-operated procurement mechanism:
- NATO allies pool financial contributions to purchase weapons from U.S. stockpiles at FMS prices
- U.S. is the "seller" / procurement agent; allies bear the cost
- **Distinct from PDA:** Does not draw from U.S. appropriated funds; allies reimburse
- **Distinct from USAI:** Does not require new U.S. contract awards; draws from existing stocks
- **First packages approved September 16–17:** 2×$500M ($1B total)
- **Participating nations:** Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Canada, Belgium, Latvia (7 nations)
- **Committed funds:** >$2B; program ceiling estimated at $10B
- **Contents confirmed:** Patriot air defense interceptors, HIMARS-compatible munitions
- **Trump signed off via:** Under Secretary of Defense for Policy Elbridge Colby

*Sources:* Reuters/Kyiv Independent (https://kyivindependent.com/trump-administration-approves-first-ukraine-arms-aid-funded-by-nato-allies-reuters-reports/), The Hill (https://thehill.com/policy/defense/5506847-ukraine-receives-lethal-aid/), CBS News (https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/white-house-nearly-1-billion-dollars-military-assistance-ukraine), Deccan Herald (https://www.deccanherald.com/world/zelenskyy-says-air-defence-missiles-included-in-first-us-supplies-under-purl-3733059). *Confidence: HIGH.*
