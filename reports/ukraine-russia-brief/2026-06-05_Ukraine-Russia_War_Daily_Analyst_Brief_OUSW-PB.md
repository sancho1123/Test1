# PART I — SENIOR LEADER SNAPSHOT

## A. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT

- **[DIPLOMATIC]** President Zelensky issued an open letter to Putin on **June 4** proposing an immediate full ceasefire along current frontlines, a face-to-face bilateral summit in a neutral country, and U.S.-monitored negotiations. The Kremlin responded that Putin would meet Zelensky in Moscow "any time" — but only to finalize a pre-agreed deal, effectively rejecting precondition-free talks.  
  Source: [France 24, "Zelensky Proposes Face-to-Face Meeting, 'Full Ceasefire' in Open Letter to Putin," June 4, 2026](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20260604-zelensky-in-open-letter-to-putin-proposes-face-to-face-meeting-and-full-ceasefire)  
  **Confidence: HIGH**

- **[AIR DEFENSE / CRITICAL SHORTAGE]** Ukraine is receiving only **60–65 Patriot interceptors per month** — a supply rate Russia's ballistic missile campaign has exceeded in just 10 days. Ukraine is in a **"critical state of vulnerability."** Zelensky is requesting U.S. licenses to **domestically manufacture** Patriot missiles.  
  Source: [United24 / Zelensky CBS News, "'60 Missiles a Month Is Nothing': Zelenskyy Asks US for Patriot Production Licenses"](https://united24media.com/war-in-ukraine/zelenskyy-says-60-65-patriot-missiles-a-month-is-nothing-asks-us-to-let-ukraine-produce-them-19354)  
  **Confidence: HIGH**

- **[STOCKPILES / EPIC FURY LINKAGE]** Operation Epic Fury (launched Feb. 28, 2026) depleted approximately **43–57% of U.S. global Patriot PAC-3 stockpiles** (1,060–1,430 missiles fired from ~2,500 inventory) and approximately **80% of U.S. THAAD interceptors** (~290 of 360 fired). Replenishment projected: **late 2030 or early 2031**.  
  Source: [CSIS, "Last Rounds? Status of Key Munitions at the Iran War Ceasefire," 2026](https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire)  
  **Confidence: HIGH**

- **[BATTLEFIELD]** **224 combat engagements** on June 4; Pokrovsk sector most intense (33 Russian assaults). Russia conducted a mass drone/missile strike June 1–2 against Kyiv killing at least 22 civilians and injuring 130. Russia is exploiting Ukraine's interceptor shortage to intensify aerial bombardment.  
  Source: [ISW June 4, 2026](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/77550) | [CNN June 4, 2026](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/06/04/europe/russia-air-attacks-ukraine-missile-defense-intl)  
  **Confidence: HIGH**

- **[FUNDING / CONGRESSIONAL]** FY2026 NDAA set USAI at **$400M** — down from ~$14B in the April 2024 supplemental. **No new PDA packages** have been issued by the Trump administration. $400M flows through EUCOM per SecDef direction. Congress imposed a new **48-hour notification requirement** if the administration suspends intelligence support to Ukraine.  
  Source: [Defense-Aerospace, "How the 2026 NDAA Scales Back U.S. Funding for Ukraine," 2026](https://www.defense-aerospace.com/how-the-2026-ndaa-scales-back-u-s-funding-for-ukraine-and-shifts-the-burden-to-europe/)  
  **Confidence: HIGH**

  *Likely RFI: When will the PAC-3 contract translate to deliverable interceptors? What is the outlay profile?*

- **[USAI / Funding / Execution]** $400M USAI is procurement-only (not drawdown). No active PDA. Ukraine won't see delivery from FY2026 USAI until FY2027 at earliest.  
  *Likely RFI: What is the execution rate on the $400M USAI? Are there obligation or delivery constraints?*

- **[DIB / Production]** Army 155mm projectile facility (General Dynamics) **failing to meet production goals** — unable to produce operational shells as of early 2026; facing potential contract termination. Propellant production up only 24% vs. 178% shell growth.  
  *Likely RFI: Is General Dynamics in breach of contract? What is the impact on Ukraine and U.S. Army readiness?*

- **[Congressional RFI / Defense Articles]** Zelensky's request for a U.S. license to produce Patriot missiles in Ukraine is **unprecedented** — likely to generate HASC/SASC oversight interest.  
  *Likely RFI: Has the administration evaluated Ukraine's Patriot production license request? What are the ITAR/EAR implications?*

- **[Epic Fury / Allies / Readiness]** DoD notified UK, Poland, Lithuania, Estonia of "significant delays" in contracted weapon deliveries due to U.S. replenishment priorities.  
  *Likely RFI: Which allied contracts are delayed due to Epic Fury priorities? Has Congress been notified?*

- **[Escalation / North Korea / Congressional]** DPRK troops rotating through Russia; 6,000 casualties per South Korea NIS. Kim Jong Un dedicated war memorial April 2026 — signaling long-term commitment.

## 6. OPERATION EPIC FURY / IRAN — UKRAINE–RUSSIA WAR LINKAGE

### A. Weapons and Stockpile Competition — CONFIRMED

- **Patriot PAC-3 MSE:** Both Ukraine and Operation Epic Fury drew from the same production line and same global U.S. inventory. Epic Fury consumed 43–57% of U.S. stock; Ukraine now receives only 60–65/month; U.S. replenishment takes explicit priority. **[CONFIRMED — HIGH]** — [CSIS](https://www.csis.org/analysis/last-rounds-status-key-munitions-iran-war-ceasefire) | [Military Times](https://www.militarytimes.com/news/your-military/2026/03/06/race-of-attrition-us-militarys-finite-interceptor-stockpile-is-being-tested/)
- **THAAD:** ~80% of U.S. global THAAD expended in Epic Fury. Japan THAAD delivery re-sequenced. **[CONFIRMED — HIGH]**
- **Tomahawk / ATACMS (indirect):** Compete for same propulsion/guidance industrial base. No confirmed direct diversion from Ukraine. **[ANALYST JUDGMENT — MEDIUM]**
- **Allied delivery delays:** Pentagon notified UK, Poland, Lithuania, Estonia of "significant delays." **[MEDIUM — reported, not officially confirmed by DoD]**

### B. Drone and Missile Warfare Linkage

- **Iranian Shahed supply to Russia disrupted:** Epic Fury damaged Iran's military-industrial capacity; future Shahed shipments likely reduced. Russian Geran-2 domestic production at Kupol (Izhevsk) continues. **[HIGH]** — [Soufan Center](https://thesoufancenter.org/intelbrief-march-5b/)
- **Iranian drone launches dropped:** One-way attack drone launches decreased as Epic Fury ramped up. **[HIGH]** — [DefenseScoop, March 4, 2026](https://defensescoop.com/2026/03/04/iranian-attack-drone-launches-decrease-operation-epic-fury/)
- **⚠ Leaked GRU document — 5,000 fiber-optic FPV drones proposed for Iran:** Would reverse the prior technology flow direction. **CANNOT CONFIRM — single leaked document, no official U.S. verification.** — [The Defense News](https://www.thedefensenews.com/news-details/Leaked-GRU-Document-Shows-Russia-Proposed-Supplying-Iran-with-5000-Fiber-Optic-Drones-and-Training-for-Potential-Use-Against-U.S.-Forces/)

### C. Russia–Iran Relationship Post-Epic Fury

- Russian assistance to Iran: limited to ammunition and intelligence per reporting. Kremlin stated no formal request received. **[MEDIUM]**
- Epic Fury has degraded Iran's capacity to supply Russia — indirect benefit to Ukraine. **[Analyst Judgment]**
- Russia benefits from U.S. strategic bandwidth compression caused by Epic Fury. **[Analyst Judgment — not confirmed in single authoritative source]**

### D. Energy and Economic Effects

- Operation Epic Fury caused what the IEA described as "the largest oil supply disruption in the history of the global oil market" (Strait of Hormuz effects). **[MEDIUM — IEA via secondary reporting]**
- Epic Fury's ~$25B cost adds pressure on FY2026/FY2027 DoD budgets — compressing space for Ukraine support.

### E. Strategic Bandwidth — CENTCOM vs. EUCOM

**Confirmed:** DoD formally re-sequenced allied weapons deliveries in favor of U.S. replenishment priorities — documented CENTCOM vs. EUCOM tradeoff. **[HIGH]**

**Analyst Judgment:** Congressional appetite for Ukraine funding faces additional compression from Epic Fury's $25B cost and the Trump administration's pre-existing posture. The $400M USAI is likely Ukraine's FY2026 ceiling unless Congress acts independently.

## 11. ANALYST ASSESSMENT

**Confirmed Facts:**
- Ukraine faces a documented, severe Patriot interceptor shortage (60–65/month vs. Russian ballistic missile pace)
- Operation Epic Fury directly depleted the same U.S. Patriot inventory Ukraine depends on
- The FY2026 NDAA provides only $400M USAI — a fraction of historical supplemental support
- DPRK continues military participation with long-term domestic propaganda commitment
- China is the dominant enabler of Russia's dual-use supply chain (~90% of high-priority goods)
- Replenishment of critical U.S. air defense assets will not complete before late 2030

**Analyst Judgment:**
- **Russia's strategic position is favorable in the near term.** Its offensive has stalled territorially but it is imposing mass damage via aerial bombardment with minimal air defense cost. Reduced U.S. funding, depleted stockpiles, and reduced PDA appetite create the best Russian strategic environment since early 2022.
- **The interceptor gap is the single most critical near-term vulnerability.** The 2–3 year production ramp creates a structural 2026–2028 window where Ukraine's air defense will remain critically constrained regardless of political decisions.
- **Operation Epic Fury's indirect effects on Ukraine are significant and underreported.** The stockpile competition, allied delivery delays, and bandwidth compression are a tangible drag on Ukraine support that occurs automatically — no explicit policy decision required.
- **The Zelensky ceasefire letter is a significant diplomatic marker.** Congress may use it to question whether current support levels are necessary if Ukraine itself proposes ceasefire on current frontlines.

**Cannot Confirm:**
- GRU fiber-optic drone transfer proposal to Iran (single leaked document)
- Exact Tomahawk replenishment timeline (classified)
- Whether GD 155mm facility will face contract termination or recovery
- Whether Trump administration will issue any new PDA for Ukraine in FY2026
