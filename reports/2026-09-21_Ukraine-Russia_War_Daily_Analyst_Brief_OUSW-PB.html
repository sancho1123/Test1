3C. Stockpile and Readiness Issues

CRITICALHIGH

The U.S. Patriot PAC-3 production-consumption gap is the defining readiness constraint. Lockheed Martin delivered ~620 interceptors in 2025 (~1.7/day globally) against a network of dozens of batteries worldwide. In the first 16 days of Operation Epic Fury alone, 402 Patriot interceptors were fired. Even at the ambitious 2,000/year target (2030), two simultaneous high-intensity campaigns would deplete that production in weeks. This is not a procurement line item problem — it is a structural industrial base problem requiring sustained MYP investment.

6. Operation Epic Fury / Iran — Ukraine–Russia Linkage

CONFIRMED LINKAGEHIGH

Interceptor competition is the central confirmed cross-theater linkage. Both Operation Epic Fury and Ukraine's air defense are drawing from the same U.S. Patriot and THAAD inventory. Washington's explicit prioritization of CENTCOM/INDOPACOM reconstitution over EUCOM resupply is documented. The U.S. cannot currently supply both theaters at scale given production constraints. This is the most significant concrete linkage for OUSW comptroller purposes.

Defense News Jul 30 2026 · CSIS 2026 · EuroMaidan Press Apr 22 2026
ENERGY LINKAGEHIGH

Operation Epic Fury generated a major oil price shock that initially benefited Russia's budget. Brent surged to ~$118 in late March 2026 when OEF began, driving Russian oil/gas revenues from ~$12B/month to ~$24B/month in March. By September, prices have rebounded to ~$109 after a mid-year correction to $70. However, Ukraine's and allies' attacks on Russian Baltic and Black Sea export infrastructure have cut physical export volumes by up to 40%, partially neutralizing the price gain. Net effect on Russian budget is "meager" compared to initial expectations (Moscow Times, Sep 1, 2026).

Chatham House Apr 2026 · Moscow Times Sep 1 2026

Operation Epic Fury has struck Iran's military infrastructure, likely disrupting — but not eliminating — Iranian drone and missile supply to Russia. Iran's ability to manufacture and deliver Shahed drones and ballistic missiles to Russia is degraded under active U.S. airstrikes. North Korea is filling the gap.

ANALYST JUDGMENT
  • Most important issue today: The intersection of Ukrainian air-defense collapse and Russian continued ballistic campaign is the most urgent operational-to-policy gap. Without Patriot interceptors and with Russia deploying training missiles and NK ballistic missiles to saturate air defenses, Ukraine faces unprecedented vulnerability to infrastructure destruction.
  • Next 7 days: Watch for post-Duma Kremlin signals; IG report congressional fallout; any emergency European or U.S. interceptor transfer decisions; NK troop expansion confirmation.
